Back up your data
📦
Download a copy of your vault
Your policies and uploaded documents will be saved as a single file on your device. Keep it somewhere safe — you can use it to restore your data on any browser or device.
What's included
✓ All your policy details and notes
✓ Uploaded policy documents and renewal notices
✓ Coverage notes and renewal history
🔒
Your data is saved locally on your device only and is never sent to any server. We recommend storing your backup file in an encrypted folder or password-protected drive.
Restore from backup
📂
Restore your policies from a file
Select a backup file you previously downloaded from PolicyVault. Your policies and documents will be restored exactly as they were.
How it works
1 Select your backup file (ends in .json)
2 PolicyVault reads the file and restores your policies and documents
3 Existing policies are kept — only new or updated ones are added
⚠️
Only use backup files downloaded from PolicyVault. Files from other sources may not work correctly. If a policy in the backup has the same ID as an existing one, it will be overwritten.
